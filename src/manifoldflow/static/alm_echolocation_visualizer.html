Glossary

What ALM receives

The sensory scene presented at each moment. The dot and target disappear when vision is removed, while the event chimes continue. The green estimate and controls are overlays—not sensory inputs.

Organism development

A live, sampled view of the visual, auditory, and integration organs taking shape. During learning, genuine developmental snapshots are slowed to human speed: new relations emerge and settle while the round, relation count, and visible-change count advance. These are observed structural changes, not decorative motion. Outlines show which organs are active.

Raw sensory vector

The numerical channels arriving at the organism: visible position and target evidence, whether the sense is available, and discrete ranging and crossing events. No distance, velocity, or cadence value is supplied.

Frequently asked questions

A plain-language guide to what this experiment establishes, what the controls mean, and where the result should remain deliberately modest.

What is the echolocation experiment?

A moving dot approaches and crosses a target while a ranging chime changes cadence with proximity and a separate event marks crossing. During training, vision and sound are both available. In held-out evaluation, vision disappears while sound continues, forcing the system to estimate the hidden position from its retained state and acoustic events.

What does a successful result show?

It shows that temporal sound structure learned alongside vision can carry useful spatial information after vision is removed. Dead-reckoning, shuffled-sound, and silent controls test whether the result depends on the real organization of the sound rather than trajectory averages or memory alone.

What does the green circle mean?

It is a diagnostic estimate of the dot’s position, overlaid for human inspection. It is not a command, reward, navigation policy, or input returned to the organism. In dead-reckoning mode, the same marker displays the constant-velocity baseline and is labeled DR.

What are the three organs?

Vision develops around visual evidence, audition around event sound, and integration combines their useful state. This is differentiated anatomy rather than three separately trained models. The division supplies an innate structural bias without prescribing the concepts each organ must learn.

What does Learning on/off do?

In the continuous playground, learning on displays the representation being developed from the reversing experience. Its actual developmental snapshots are replayed slowly enough to inspect. Turning learning off restores the original trained visual organ while inference, motion, and sound continue; turning it back on returns to the adapted organ.

How does ALM learn without backpropagation?

ALM develops from locally available activity and interaction rather than sending a global error gradient backward through a stack of layers. Learning occurs where evidence is encountered, using the same general developmental machinery across organs. A diagnostic readout makes the resulting state measurable, but it is not the mechanism that creates the internal sensory relations.

How is this different from transformers?

A transformer is ordinarily trained by optimizing a global prediction loss through backpropagation, then uses attention over its available context. ALM is natively stream-oriented: temporal state and local structure develop as observations arrive. This experiment compares learning routes, not a claim that either architecture wins every narrowly defined task.

How is this different from traditional embodied AI?

Many embodied systems begin with engineered state estimators, task-specific perception modules, explicit rewards, or labeled quantities such as distance and velocity. Here the organism receives raw low-dimensional sensory evidence and discrete events, then develops a shared internal state without being handed those hidden quantities. This remains a controlled synthetic world, not yet a robotics demonstration.

What does ALM-64MB-1C mean?

It names the organism’s body class: a 64 MB carrying-capacity ceiling and one developmental lineage. The live grown-byte count reports how much structure this particular exposure actually produced. The class is a capacity limit, not a promise that every run fills the entire body.

What should we not conclude yet?

This does not establish general intelligence, autonomous navigation, biological equivalence, or robustness in unrestricted environments. It is evidence that a backprop-free local architecture can form and reuse a cross-modal temporal representation in a carefully controlled task. Broader composition and transfer remain open research questions.
