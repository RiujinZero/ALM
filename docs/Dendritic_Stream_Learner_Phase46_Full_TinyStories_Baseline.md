# Phase 46: Full TinyStories Exposure Baseline

## Result

The compacted motif/event cortex completed one raw-byte pass over the full
1,924,281,556-byte TinyStories training file and scored the complete
19,447,282-byte validation file. This checkpoint is an exposure-only control:
the process was launched before the Phase 41--45 long-range gates were built.
It includes the earlier receptor-routed contextual residuals, six-byte sequence
paths, two-word event paths, motif boundaries, and nursery shedding. It does
not include addressed apical credit, learned communication routing,
actor/patient role binding, adaptive eligibility, or recurrent event tags.

| Measure | Full-exposure baseline |
|---|---:|
| Training bytes | 1,924,281,556 |
| Validation bytes | 19,447,282 |
| Training bpb | 1.374430 |
| Validation bpb | 1.135893 |
| Learner-compute training rate | 449,314 B/s |
| End-to-end territory rate | 402,370 B/s |
| Validation rate | 23,491 B/s |
| Consolidation time | 501.18 s |
| Embodied bytes | 491,512,337 |
| Checkpoint file | 491,517,223 bytes |

The observed consolidated coordinator occupied about 5.15 GiB, approximately
11.2 times the durable body. During parallel learning the coordinator plus
twelve roughly 0.42GiB territory workers occupied about 9.8GiB, approximately
21 times the eventual body. These are observed Python/runtime footprints, not
a claim that continual plasticity intrinsically requires those ratios. Object
headers, hash tables, simultaneous territory bodies, merge copies, and
temporary scoring state are optimization targets.

The resulting deployment distinction is useful:

- a plastic organism may retain bounded nurseries, eligibility, route evidence,
  and online homeostatic state; and
- a frozen descendant may retain only mature predictive anatomy.

Always-on learning still requires bounded local consolidation during wakeful
operation. Rare deeper sleep can remain an optional reorganization mechanism,
not the only way to reclaim memory.

## Likelihood Versus Anatomy

Compared with the 300MB Phase 39 checkpoint, full exposure reduced validation
bpb by only 0.01923 while growing durable anatomy by 3.25 times.

| Checkpoint | Exposure | Validation bpb | Embodied bytes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Phase 39 | 300,000,000 | 1.155121 | 151,382,898 |
| Phase 46 control | 1,924,281,556 | 1.135893 | 491,512,337 |

That is a real predictive improvement, but it is not a compelling marginal
memory trade by itself. Free-running behavior is the more important test of
whether the additional anatomy represents durable higher-order structure.

## Matched Generation Sweep

Both checkpoints were prompted with `Once upon a time` and sampled for at most
1,000 raw bytes across seeds 0--9 with temperature 0.8, top-k 32, top-p 0.9,
and stopping at the learned story boundary.

| Checkpoint | Boundary completion | Mean generated bytes | Mean zlib ratio | Mean distinct 4-grams |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Phase 39, 300MB | 80% | 442.5 | 0.7121 | 0.8037 |
| Phase 46, full | 60% | 722.5 | 0.5652 | 0.6959 |

All outputs were valid UTF-8 and printable. The full checkpoint generated
recognizable TinyStories vocabulary, syntax, dialogue, and local story
scaffolds, but continued to swap actors, pronouns, and objects and to splice
unrelated events. Its greedy 2,000-byte continuation entered an exact phrase
cycle, with a 0.1345 zlib ratio, 0.1502 distinct four-gram fraction, and no
story boundary.

A representative stochastic continuation begins:

> Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Timmy. Timmy loved to go
> outside and play with the moral of the way. She looked at the scale...

This is not gibberish, but the immediate identity change demonstrates that
local fluency is not stable event coherence. The ten-seed sample is too small
to estimate a precise population failure rate, and length affects the
repetition diagnostics, but it gives no evidence that full exposure repaired
the Phase 39 long-range failure. Lower bpb and worse boundary/repetition
behavior can coexist.

## Next Gate

Do not buy another full-corpus pass with capacity alone. Integrate the validated
long-range mechanisms into a smaller 300MB ablation first:

1. bind learned content assemblies to reusable actor, action, patient,
   location, goal, and outcome receptors rather than unordered coactivity;
2. let context-route branches select the relevant recurrent event column;
3. retain event identity in bounded recurrent tags or temporal-basis
   assemblies instead of a lossy scalar trace;
4. deliver downstream surprise as receiver-addressed apical instruction while
   computing each synaptic update locally; and
5. consolidate mature columns online under fixed nursery and route-evidence
   byte budgets.

The integration must beat this control simultaneously on held-out bpb,
boundary-completed generation across seeds, role/event consistency, and awake
working-set bytes. Only then should it receive another full TinyStories pass.
